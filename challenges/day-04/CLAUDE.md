# Day 4 — Analytics dashboard: funnel + retention view

## Brief
A growth team checks this every Monday. They need to see where people drop out of signup and whether the ones who stay come back. The current version shows the numbers, but does it tell the story?

## Constraints
- One screen, desktop first (1440), must still read at 390 mobile.
- WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast 4.5:1, never color as the only signal.
- Keep the data honest: same numbers, better story.

## Top crits from the community
1. Drop-off is the story. Make the biggest drop the hero and annotate why people leave there.
2. KPI labels need units and a time frame. "38%" of what, retained after which week?
3. Equal-width funnel bars hide the size of each drop. Show % lost between steps.
4. The highlighted bar is the only signal. Add a label so it works without color.
